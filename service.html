Services

Everything under one calm Baner roof

Preventive care, microscope-minded endodontics, implants, aligners, and cosmetic finishing—each treatment below links to a dedicated Baner page with a longer blog guide when you want depth. For longer reads, see our blog.

Root canal treatment Infected pulp is removed, canals are cleaned and sealed under gentle anesthesia—microscope-guided care to save your tooth, often in one or two visits. Dental implants A titanium post is placed in the jawbone; after healing, a custom crown is attached—replacing the tooth without grinding neighbours down. Dentures Impressions are taken, then a full or partial denture is made and adjusted for fit—restoring chewing and lip support; implants optional for extra hold. Invisible aligners A digital plan stages tooth movement; you wear clear trays on schedule and visit for checks—straightening without fixed braces. Dental bridges Neighbouring teeth are crowned to hold a replacement tooth in the gap; the bridge is cemented and you learn how to clean underneath. Teeth whitening Enamel and gums are checked first, then in-office bleach lifts stains—controlled whitening safer for your tissues than DIY kits. Dental scaling Tartar is removed above and below the gumline, then teeth are polished—easing bleeding gums; your next visit is set to your gum health. Composite restoration Decay or chips are removed, then composite is bonded in layers, shaped to your bite, and polished—matching enamel before decay hits the nerve. Wisdom tooth extraction Imaging maps the roots; the wisdom tooth is removed under local anesthesia with after-care instructions—when impaction or pain risks infection. Veneers Front teeth are prepared, thin veneers are bonded after a shade and shape preview—fixing chips, gaps, or stubborn colour. Zirconia crowns The tooth is shaped, a metal-free zirconia crown is made to your bite and cemented on—protecting root-treated or fractured teeth. Pedodontic treatment Child-paced exams, fluoride guidance, and gentle fillings when needed—catching decay early and teaching parents home care.
