Privacy Policy

How Enamel Craft Dental Studio handles your information.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Enamel Craft Dental Studio (“we”, “our”, “the clinic”), located at Shop No. 3, Shreeban building, Sneh Park Society, Pancard Club Rd, Baner, Pune 411045, collects and uses personal information when you visit our website, book care, or communicate with us.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: name, phone number, email address, and messages you send via our website forms, online consultation booking, WhatsApp, phone, or email.
  • Clinical information: dental history, symptoms, records, X-rays, photographs, and treatment details you provide or that we create during your care.
  • Payment information: we do not store card or UPI PIN data on our website. Payments made via Razorpay, UPI, or at the clinic are processed by those providers; we may receive confirmation references or screenshots you share.
  • Technical data: device/browser type, approximate location, and usage data through tools such as Google Tag Manager/Analytics (if enabled), to improve the site.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, provide, and follow up on dental treatment and online consultations.
  • To respond to enquiries and send appointment reminders or care instructions.
  • To maintain clinical records as required for continuity of care and applicable regulations.
  • With your consent, to use photographs or case details in anonymised or identified form for education, our website, social media, or marketing (see Terms of Use — image consent).
  • To improve our website and measure how visitors use it.

Legal basis and consent

We process personal data based on your consent, performance of treatment you request, and our legitimate interest in operating a safe dental practice. You may withdraw marketing or image-use consent at any time without affecting your clinical care; contact us below.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only when necessary with:

  • Healthcare partners you authorise (e.g. insurance or corporate health networks).
  • Service providers (email delivery, appointment tools, payment gateways, website hosting) under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities when required by law.

Data retention

Clinical records are kept in line with applicable dental council and statutory retention requirements. Marketing and website enquiry data are retained only as long as needed for the purpose collected, unless you ask us to delete it (subject to legal record-keeping duties).

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards for records and communications. No online transmission is completely secure; please avoid sending highly sensitive information except through channels we direct you to use.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold (where not prevented by law), and object to certain uses. To exercise these rights, contact us at enamelcraft.dental@gmail.com or +91-7666297468.

Children

We treat patients of all ages. For minors, we collect and use information with appropriate parental or guardian consent as required for treatment and records.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

See also our Terms of Use, including dental treatment and photograph consent.
